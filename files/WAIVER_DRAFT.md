# Waiver — working draft v0.1

**Not legal advice.** This is a structured starting point for you and a UAE-qualified lawyer to work from, and for your insurer to review. Do not publish it as-is.

## What this document is actually for

Under UAE civil law you cannot contract away liability for your own negligence — a release clause does not shield the operator the way it would under US law. So this waiver is not a liability shield. Drafted properly it is evidence of four things, all of which matter if something goes wrong:

1. The guardian was informed of the risks and accepted them
2. The guardian read and accepted the zone rules
3. The guardian declared the child fit to participate and disclosed relevant medical information
4. The guardian authorised emergency first aid and transport

Everything below serves one of those four purposes. Anything that doesn't, cut.

## Structure

### 1. Who this agreement is between
Zawaya's full legal entity name, licence number, the event, the dates, and the two zones by name.

### 2. Declaration of authority
The person signing confirms they are over 18 and are the parent or legal guardian of each child listed, with authority to sign on their behalf.

### 3. The activities and their risks
Plainly described, per zone. Soft play: falls, collisions with other children, minor impact injuries. Inflatables: falls, collisions, impact with the structure, injuries from other users. State that these are active physical play environments where minor injury is a normal possibility despite supervision.

### 4. Supervision — the clause that does the real work
- **Soft Play:** the guardian remains inside the zone and retains responsibility for direct supervision of their child at all times. Zawaya staff manage the environment and the rules; they do not replace the guardian.
- **Bouncy Castles:** the guardian may leave the zone, and by doing so confirms they will remain contactable on the mobile number provided, remain on the event premises, and return before the session end time.
- **In both:** no child leaves the zone unaccompanied. A child is released only to the registered guardian, or to another adult the guardian has authorised, verified by the registration code.

### 5. Health declaration
The child is in good health and fit for active physical play; is not currently unwell or carrying an untreated injury; and any condition, allergy or requirement relevant to their safety has been disclosed in the medical notes field.

### 6. Zone rules the guardian accepts on the child's behalf
Socks required, no shoes; no food, drink or chewing gum; no jewellery, watches, badges, hard costume pieces, glasses straps or lanyards; no sharp costume accessories or props; height and age limits as posted; staff instructions to be followed; staff may remove a child from the zone for unsafe behaviour without refund.
*Costume props deserve their own line here — this is a comic con.*

### 7. Emergency medical authorisation
Consent for Zawaya staff or event medical personnel to administer first aid and, where judged necessary, to arrange transport to a medical facility, with reasonable efforts to contact the guardian first and costs of treatment remaining the guardian's responsibility.

### 8. Personal belongings
Zawaya is not responsible for items left in or near the zone.

### 9. Photography and media — separate, optional, unticked
Consent for photography or video that may include the child, for Zawaya's marketing. Explicitly optional and refusable with no effect on entry. Must be a distinct checkbox, never bundled with the main consent.

### 10. Data protection notice (PDPL)
Written as a plain notice, not buried in a clause:
- Zawaya is the data controller, with a contact address
- What is collected: guardian name, relation, mobile, email; child name, age, and any medical note
- Why: operating the zones, contacting the guardian during and after a session, and safeguarding
- Retention: children's data deleted 30 days after the event; guardian contact details kept beyond that only where marketing consent was given
- **Cross-border:** data is stored on servers outside the UAE (AWS, India), with contractual safeguards in place. This must be disclosed
- Rights: access, correction, deletion, withdrawal of consent, and how to exercise them

### 11. Marketing consent — separate, optional
Its own unticked box with its own sentence. See PRD §8.1 for why I'd keep it unticked.

### 12. Governing law
UAE federal law, courts of Abu Dhabi.

### 13. Acceptance record
Version number and date of the waiver text, the timestamp of acceptance, and the typed full name of the guardian. Captured automatically by the system.

## Drafting notes

- **Keep it under a page on a phone.** A waiver nobody reads is weaker evidence than a short one they did. The scroll-gate only proves they scrolled; brevity is what makes reading plausible.
- **Arabic and English are equally authoritative,** or nominate one as controlling. Decide with the lawyer, and get the Arabic professionally translated rather than machine-translated — a poor Arabic version undermines the whole document.
- **Every version is archived.** A guardian who signed on day one signed v3, and the record must say so.
- **Ask your insurer directly:** does the policy require a drawn signature, and does it require anything specific in the health declaration or the supervision clause? Their answer is the fastest way to finish this document.
